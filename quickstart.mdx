Follow these steps to get access to BeelPoint.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log in to TaxProMax">
    Go to [einvoice.firs.gov.ng/login](https://einvoice.firs.gov.ng/login) and sign in with your TaxProMax credentials.

    ![Step1](/images/Step1.jpg "Step1")
  </Step>
  <Step title="Grant access to BeelPoint">
    Click **View Access Point Providers**, find **TELEPAC AFRICA NIGERIA LIMITED**, and turn on access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log in to BeelPoint">
    Go to [beelpoint.com/login](https://beelpoint.com/login) and sign in with the same TaxProMax login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If access is not granted, contact [support@telepacafrica.com](mailto:support@telepacafrica.com). Read the terms and conditions before you proceed.
</Note>
