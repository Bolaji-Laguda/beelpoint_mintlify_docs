sidebarTitle: "Invoice categories"
description: "The invoice categories BeelPoint supports."
---

BeelPoint supports three invoice categories. Set the category with the `invoiceCategory` field when you validate or sign an invoice.

| Category | Code | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Business to Business | `B2B` | Invoices issued to another business. |
| Business to Customer | `B2C` | Invoices issued to an individual customer. |
| Business to Government | `B2G` | Invoices issued to a government entity. |
